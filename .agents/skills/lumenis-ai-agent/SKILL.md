# AI / Agent
AI enhances the product; AI is not Production Truth. Route providers through the single SYSTEM AI Gateway and agents through canonical owners. Learning is derivative: it may propose/evaluate/version changes but may not silently rewrite Evidence, Canonical Truth, Permission, approvals, or effects. Require real input/result/error semantics and evidence for user-visible AI capability.

The C1–C6 owner map and retired-provider policy are in root `AGENTS.md`. Do not
call, auto-discover, probe, fall back to, or reactivate local-model or Zhipu
providers or Jev for LUMENIS or its development program; preserve their existing
interfaces and historical artifacts. Owned model training and inference belong
to C2 through existing owners and gates. Cloud workers must use the checked-in
repository route and must not assume access to workstation model providers.
