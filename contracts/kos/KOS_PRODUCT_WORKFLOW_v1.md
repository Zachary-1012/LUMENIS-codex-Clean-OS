# KOS Product Workflow Contract v1

> Scope: KOS business objects, states, fields, ownership and workflow truth only.  
> Presentation authority: `00-GOVERNANCE/LUMENIS-EXPERIENCE-CONSTITUTION.md`.

This contract defines **no independent UI/UX/visual language**.

## Business flow

```text
Collection & Quality Gate → Identity / Entity Ownership Gate
→ KOS Identification & Scoring → KOS Asset Management
→ Operations → Growth / Paid Amplification
→ Analytics & Decision → Reporting / Evaluation
```

Primary journey:

```text
collect → clean/dedupe → entity ownership → KOS identity → Evidence
→ score/tier → account asset → operation → outcome → feedback → report
```

The KOS account is the core business object. Business facets include identity, ownership Evidence, tier/score, performance, content profile, comment intelligence, lifecycle/risk, contact/lead information, cooperation history, opportunities, AI recommendations and Evidence.

Actions derive from real state: incomplete lead → complete lead; qualified/uncontacted → prepare outreach; contacted/no reply → follow-up; published → collect performance; strong organic performance → paid-amplification candidate; low-confidence/anomaly → quarantine or human review.

Generated, queued, sent, delivered, replied, accepted, completed and failed remain distinct states.

Merchant KOS, brand KOS, partner KOS, target-entity KOS, independent KOL/KOC, UGC, media, official and unknown remain semantically distinguishable. Mentioning an entity is not proof of ownership.

Supported fields may include KOS_ID, tier, account/nickname, platform, UID, profile URL, entity ownership/type/confidence, KOS type, followers, posting volume, average engagement signals, growth, content match, expertise, continuity, reach, comment handling, paid amplification potential, lifecycle, risk, tags, contact fields, MCN/company, cooperation direction, budget/quote, lead completeness, verification/contact timestamps, cooperation status, next action, AI recommendation, Evidence ID, freshness and asset health.

Unknown/unavailable values remain honest states.

Reporting lineage:

```text
account / segment → content → performance → Evidence
→ decision → action → outcome → evaluation
```

Dynamic UI and exports derive from the same truth/Evidence.
