# Global BIL Benchmark Selection v1

## Initial S/A/B benchmark set

This is a research/validation benchmark set, not a ranking of brand quality.

### S — mechanism-critical
- Patagonia — values/community + product-purpose alignment
- Jacquemus — visual world-building + cultural moments
- Glossier — UGC + consumer feedback + co-development
- Salomon — athlete/creator ecosystem + community discovery
- Nike — global culture + athlete ecosystem + global-local execution
- Miu Miu — cultural relevance + product desirability

### A — high-value capability benchmarks
- Gentle Monster — experiential retail / store-as-content
- Lululemon — community / ambassador / store loop
- On — product innovation / premium challenger
- The Ordinary — education-led beauty / trust
- Aesop — sensory retail / brand world
- Uniqlo — global-local functional retail
- Adidas — sport-culture / collaborations
- SHUSHU/TONG — Chinese designer global niche
- BY FAR — social-first fashion lifecycle
- AllSaints — heritage resurgence
- boohoo — creator-commerce attribution
- Hot Topic — fandom/community commerce

### B — specialist / cult benchmarks
- Molly Goddard — distinctive design/community/editorial

## Why these are useful

The set intentionally spans different growth mechanisms, not just famous brands. It can therefore validate whether BIL can distinguish:

1. social heat from brand health;
2. UGC volume from UGC value;
3. creator reach from creator quality;
4. cultural relevance from purchase intent;
5. global brand DNA from local execution;
6. retail experience from ordinary store presence;
7. growth from temporary virality;
8. heritage recovery from ordinary rebranding;
9. creator content from attributable commerce;
10. AI discovery visibility from traditional SEO/social visibility.

## Evidence policy

Public case-study results are treated as directional evidence, not universal causal benchmarks. Metrics must retain source, date, market, platform and measurement context before being loaded as observations.

## Current external evidence examples

- Hot Topic: creator-led storefront program reported 1,500+ creators, 31% of sale-period UGC and 10–12% AOV lift. Source: LoudCrowd case study, published January 2026.
- boohoo: creator storefront/affiliate program reported £15M revenue run-rate, 25,000+ new customers, and substantial UGC contribution. Source: LoudCrowd case study, published May 2025.
- The Noli Shop: shoppable UGC was reported to outperform brand model-style video on conversion, with results measured across 221 product pages. Source: Hue case study, 2026.
- Semrush 2026 AI Visibility Index: 126M U.S. AI-search prompts were analyzed in the expanded study, supporting AI discovery as a distinct measurement layer.

These are case-specific observations and must not be copied into brand KPIs without comparable measurement context.
