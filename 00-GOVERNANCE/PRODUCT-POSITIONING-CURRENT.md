# 序境 / LUMENIS — Current Product Positioning

## Product identity

序境 / LUMENIS is one production product for commercial operations and brand-market intelligence. AI enhances its workflows; current Product Core, Runtime, Data, Permission and Evidence remain the owners of Production Truth.

The final user-facing product is **one product with four coordinated experience surfaces**:

- **Public Website** — commercial presentation, capabilities, scenarios, cases, demo, trial application, enterprise contact, help and the login/workspace entry. It never owns Production Truth.
- **Web Workspace** — the primary browser work surface for Subject / BrandFocus selection, CTH / BMP / AI / Shared / SYSTEM workflows, Evidence Trace, Case / Insight / Decision / Action, report/export, collaboration and approval.
- **Desktop App** — the Power User client for local Browser / CDP, Cookie / Session, Collector, long-running tasks, local files, Ollama / Local AI, private deployment, Runtime, Recovery and advanced execution.
- **Mobile Companion** — notification, event and AI-result review, approval, task status, report reading and bounded quick actions.

Web Workspace, Desktop App and Mobile Companion share **ONE Product Core, ONE Canonical API / ViewModel contract, ONE Canonical Data, ONE Evidence, ONE Permission and ONE workflow meaning**. Flutter remains the approved shared application technology for Web Workspace, Desktop App and Mobile Companion; terminal-specific composition and capability boundaries are required instead of identical layouts. Public Website is separately deployable presentation and acquisition surface, not a second product or business-logic owner.

The approved application targets remain Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS; target presence is not release evidence, and each platform still requires its own runtime and release gate.

Generated PDFs, audit illustrations, screenshots, mockups and Figma explorations are `DESIGN_EXPLORATION`, not implementation authority. A screen may be implemented only after the current Experience Constitution and its detailed screen specification record a Sol-reviewed `DESIGN_READY` decision.

## Four product domains

| Domain | Positioning | Product responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CTH · Commercial Operations | Subject-first | Subjects, venues and projects, events, cases, risk, decisions and actions |
| BMP · Brand and Market | Brand/Market-first | Brands, competitors, creators and accounts, campaigns, communication performance, benchmarks, risk and evaluation |
| Shared Capability | Shared across CTH and BMP | Content and creation, Evidence, research, evaluation, learning assets and AI enhancement |
| SYSTEM | Product control and infrastructure | Collection, Runtime, Scheduler, AI Gateway, Database, Security, Permission, Settings, Recovery and Observability |

CTH and BMP may share Subject Context and infrastructure. They retain separate domain models, metrics, relationships, decisions and action semantics. Shared Capability serves both domains and does not become a third business product. SYSTEM protects and operates the product; it does not redefine either domain.
