# ROUND 06 — public article extraction and data-quality boundary

## Accepted source slice

The existing SYSTEM `PublicHttpAdapter` keeps a fetched page's same-scheme,
same-host canonical identity, rejects cross-host and HTTPS-downgrade hints, and carries bounded
transport provenance. When a substantial article/body container exists, it
captures that text instead of an unrelated publisher-wide meta description;
an explicit `PubDate` meta value is retained. This remains a bounded public
HTML adapter, not a new media/social collector, metric owner or license grant.

are not collected product data or an approved global inclusion rule.
