# 寰意 / OEVIRA — Current Product Positioning

## 2026-10-18 当前品牌陈述与两类受众

**寰意 / OEVIRA** · **An Open Space for Thought and Creation.** · **oevira.com**

我们从使用产品的人出发。技术应该解决什么，取决于他们为什么来到这里。

对于品牌方、商业运营者和代理机构，产品需要承担实际的工作。一份研究要能帮助团队作出判断，形成的方案也要能够继续修改、执行和复盘。资料是否可靠，成果能否真正使用，比系统给出了多少答案更重要。

对于普通人，关注可以只是因为喜欢。一个品牌、一座常去的商业空间，或者一家感兴趣的公司，都值得被好好了解。我们希望，人们不必反复搜索、拼凑消息，也不必先想好该问什么，打开产品便能逐渐发现更多与自己有关的事情。

专业的需要与个人的喜爱，是 OEVIRA 希望服务的两种起点。它们不必采用相同的使用方式，却应当得到同样认真的对待。

OEVIRA 正从商业运营、品牌市场与研究创作的专业工作中建立这些能力。独立的想法同样可以成为工作的开始，一份文稿、一段影像或一期播客，不需要先经过一轮商业分析。服务普通大众，是这一产品继续发展的愿景，而不是对尚未实现功能的承诺。

我们相信，信任需要在使用中获得。重要的判断应当能够回到依据，无法确认的事情应当保留它的不确定。AI 可以帮助理解和创造，但不代替事实，也不替人作出最后的选择。

「寰」是世界的广阔，「意」是每个人放在心上的事。「芥子纳须弥」给予这个名字的启发，是一个细微的发现，也可能通向更大的世界。

我们希望，当人们逐渐熟悉 OEVIRA，会自然地觉得：

**原来我一直想要这样一个地方，可以好好了解自己真正关心的事。**

## 2026-10-11 最新 PO 定位校正

寰意是面向商业运营与品牌市场的证据驱动工作平台，保持 CTH/BMP/Shared/SYSTEM 与五轮架构。AI 在各领域增强理解、建议、生成和任务辅助，但普通业务无AI仍完成；第五轮是增强/模型/跨域终验收，不是AI首次进入产品。后端负责数据分析和数学方法，用户获得已分析报告与可操作成果，不承担论文算法配置。

CTH/BMP是业务领域，Shared内容制作是独立可用的制作能力。用户可以直接创建和编辑提案/文稿、视频、播客，不必先完成业务分析或创建业务域任务；也可按需要采用业务/研究资料。共享表示统一复用能力，不表示依赖业务领域才能运行。

通用电脑/OS、任意安装应用及远程桌面控制已撤出产品要求，不列后置欠项、不记完成。产品内受控浏览器、后台采集、正常文件/媒体处理、权限与保存恢复继续保留；历史源码和研究不删除。具体计划只归 Product Architecture，呈现只归 Experience；窗口调度不属于产品定位；Public Website、Web Workspace、Windows/macOS Desktop与iOS Mobile按既有角色并行建设、分别验收；一端未完成不阻止其他端独立工作。

## Product identity

寰意 / OEVIRA is one production product for commercial operations and brand-market intelligence. AI enhances its workflows; current Product Core, Runtime, Data, Permission and Evidence remain the owners of Production Truth.

The final user-facing product is **one product with four coordinated experience surfaces**:

- **Public Website** — commercial presentation, capabilities, scenarios, cases, demo, trial application, enterprise contact, help and the login/workspace entry. It never owns Production Truth.
- **Web Workspace** — the primary browser work surface for Subject / BrandFocus selection, CTH / BMP / AI / Shared / SYSTEM workflows, Evidence Trace, Case / Insight / Decision / Action, report/export, collaboration and approval.
- **Desktop App** — the Power User client for local Browser / CDP, Cookie / Session, Collector, long-running tasks, local files, approved owned AI capability, private deployment, Runtime, Recovery and advanced execution.
- **Mobile Companion** — notification, event and AI-result review, approval, task status, report reading and bounded quick actions.

Web Workspace, Desktop App and Mobile Companion share **ONE Product Core, ONE Canonical API / ViewModel contract, ONE Canonical Data, ONE Evidence, ONE Permission and ONE workflow meaning**. Flutter remains the approved shared application technology for Web Workspace, Desktop App and Mobile Companion; terminal-specific composition and capability boundaries are required instead of identical layouts. Public Website is separately deployable presentation and acquisition surface, not a second product or business-logic owner.

Public Website, Web Workspace, Windows/macOS Desktop and iOS Mobile are built in parallel and accepted independently under their approved roles. No surface must wait for another surface to close. Native application targets remain Windows/macOS Desktop and iOS Mobile only. Android and Linux native clients are removed from active delivery requirements; preserve their existing source and history. Linux servers/CI, browser access on supported operating systems and narrow-screen Web accessibility remain in scope. Target presence is not release evidence; each retained surface still needs its applicable runtime and release gate.

Generated PDFs, audit illustrations, screenshots, mockups and Figma explorations are `DESIGN_EXPLORATION`, not implementation authority. Implement the current Experience Constitution and detailed screen specifications directly in existing frontend source, maintaining necessary decisions alongside code. A separate design draft, Figma file or M `DESIGN_READY` review is not an implementation prerequisite. Preserve existing editable design assets and honest design-review records; compilation, real operation, accessibility, security and full user-outcome acceptance remain required.

## AI-independent product operation

All normal business capabilities must complete without a model, external AI API, GPU or inference service. AI enhances but never owns Canonical facts. It is not confined to chat; the existing chat capability remains in scope. Pure AI actions may be UNAVAILABLE; deterministic work, manual decisions/editing, saving, exporting, reopening and recovery remain operable. Actual missing inputs, rights and Permission denials stay honest. Accept the no-AI business outcome first, measure AI benefits separately, and train only under its separate authorization. Exact no-AI task coverage remains in the sole Product Architecture and Real Delivery Overlay.

## Four product domains

| Domain | Positioning | Product responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CTH · Commercial Operations | Subject-first | Subjects, venues and projects, events, cases, risk, decisions and actions |
| BMP · Brand and Market | Brand/Market-first | Brands, competitors, creators and accounts, campaigns, communication performance, benchmarks, risk and evaluation |
| Shared Capability | Independently usable; shared across CTH and BMP | Content and creation, Evidence, research, evaluation, learning assets and AI enhancement |
| SYSTEM | Product control and infrastructure | Collection, Runtime, Scheduler, AI Gateway, Database, Security, Permission, Settings, Recovery and Observability |

CTH and BMP may share Subject Context and infrastructure. They retain separate domain models, metrics, relationships, decisions and action semantics. Shared Capability serves both domains and does not become a third business product. SYSTEM protects and operates the product; it does not redefine either domain.
