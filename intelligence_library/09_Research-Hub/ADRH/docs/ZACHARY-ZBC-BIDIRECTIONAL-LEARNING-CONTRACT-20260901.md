# Zachary AI ↔ ZBC Bidirectional Learning Contract

## Final positioning

Zachary AI is an independent, potentially commercialized/productized AI. Before independent launch, ZBC is its first cultivation environment, first real-use environment, and first business training environment.

Zachary operates inside ZBC but does not belong to ZBC. ZBC cultivates Zachary without defining Zachary's ultimate scope.

## Bidirectional relationship

```text
                 ZACHARY AI
          independent complete AI
                  ↕  ↕
general learning ↔ validated feedback
                  ↕  ↕
              ZBC PLATFORM
       data / cases / domain evidence
```

### ZBC → Zachary

ZBC provides approved ZBC-domain data, evidence, cases, system knowledge, operational results, and real-world feedback. These are training/cultivation inputs for Zachary's ZBC-domain capability.

### Zachary → ZBC

Zachary's useful discoveries, validated research, improved reasoning patterns, reusable capabilities, product/interaction improvements, engineering improvements, and applicable research findings may be fed back to ZBC through an explicit Evidence → Capability → Contract → Governance → Promotion path.

Zachary must not directly rewrite ZBC authoritative rules or production data merely because it learned something. Feedback is proposed, evidenced, evaluated, governed, then promoted.

## Learning separation

Zachary has two simultaneously active scopes:

1. **General Learning:** Zachary continuously studies AI, engineering, products, UX, research, industry, business, cases, tools, and other domains.
2. **ZBC Domain Learning:** the ZBC workspace learns only information relevant to ZBC's system, business, data, evidence, and cases.

General learning can produce candidates for ZBC, but ZBC promotion requires explicit applicability evidence and governance approval.

## Anti-pollution boundary

```text
ZBC Data → Zachary Context
Zachary Research → Candidate Evidence
Candidate Evidence → Evaluation
Evaluation → Contract
Contract → Governance Gate
Governance Allow → ZBC Promotion
```

No direct uncontrolled bidirectional writes. No silent rule mutation. No deletion of historical evidence when a capability or supplement expires. Domain ownership remains explicit.

## Real-time evolution

When Zachary is running within ZBC, it is the **complete Zachary AI**, not a reduced ZBC-specific model. Its general learning and validated capabilities remain available subject to governance and context boundaries.

Its ZBC-specific behavior is enriched continuously by approved ZBC evidence and real-use results. New Zachary capabilities that are demonstrably useful to ZBC can return through the same governed promotion path.

## Capability reuse

A capability discovered in any Zachary product or research stream is not automatically global. It becomes shared only after:

- evidence exists;
- ownership is identified;
- Contract is defined;
- security impact is evaluated;
- regression coverage exists;
- Governance Gate passes.

## Non-negotiable rule

**ZBC养育Zachary；Zachary反哺ZBC。两者独立、双向学习、受控共享、不可互相污染。**

This contract supplements the existing Zachary AI consolidation baseline and must not replace it.
