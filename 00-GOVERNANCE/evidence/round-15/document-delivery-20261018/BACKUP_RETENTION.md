# Product archive custody

Original code, documents and unredacted failure evidence remain in their existing private custody. The public candidate retains product research and sanitized test evidence, with original and published SHA-256 values distinguished. Product archive checksums are recorded in PRODUCT_ARCHIVE_CUSTODY_CHECKSUMS.json. Private storage links, user-supplied originals, conversation continuity and backup-control instructions are excluded. No original was deleted or changed.

Historical successful and failed logs describe only their recorded versions. Documentation assembly does not run tests, release the product, or establish current user acceptance. Recording collection is outside this candidate; existing ASR, subtitles, podcast and authorized voice import remain.
