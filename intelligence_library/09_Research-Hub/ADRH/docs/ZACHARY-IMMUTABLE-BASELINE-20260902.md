# Zachary AI / ZBC Immutable Baseline — 2026-09-02

## Identity
Zachary AI is an independent, complete AI product with eventual independent commercialization/productization. Before independent launch, ZBC is its first cultivation, real-use and business training environment. Zachary operates inside ZBC but does not belong to it. ZBC remains independent.

## Bidirectional learning
ZBC → Zachary through approved domain data, cases, evidence, system knowledge and real-use results.

Zachary → ZBC through validated research, capabilities, reasoning improvements, UX/product improvements and engineering improvements, but only through Evidence → Evaluation → Contract → Governance → Promotion.

Zachary continues broad general learning. ZBC-domain learning remains restricted to ZBC-related knowledge, evidence, data and cases.

## Consolidation invariants
DCC + SICI-Core + Research-Hub are the Zachary consolidation sources. ZBC is not absorbed into that three-repository core. Shared capabilities require explicit Owner + Contract/Adapter.

## Governance / evidence invariants
Governance authorizes; Execution executes. Evidence must distinguish expected from observed state. Unknown states remain NOT_VERIFIED and cannot be called PASS without executable evidence. Baseline is authoritative; Supplements are additive and lifecycle-controlled. Historical evidence remains auditable.

## Functional invariants
Zachary must remain complete; consolidation may not produce a lightweight edition. Mobile-first is the current delivery direction.

## Security invariants
No credentials, tokens, passwords or secrets in source-controlled docs/frontend. Least privilege is mandatory. SICI security boundaries remain unchanged. Cross-repository changes must be bounded, reversible and auditable.

## Change rule
Identity, ownership, security, evidence and governance invariants require an explicit baseline-change decision with evidence, impact analysis, migration/rollback plan and human approval.
