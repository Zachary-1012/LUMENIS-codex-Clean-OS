# Podcast Episode Reverse Engineering & Coverage Protocol V1

## Purpose
Build auditable episode-level research for StoryFM, 凉子访谈录 and future benchmark podcasts without pretending that partial analysis equals complete coverage.

## Coverage Ledger
Each canonical episode gets one row/record with:
CASE_ID, program, episode_number_if_verified, title, date, duration, canonical_source, alternate_sources, metadata_status, transcript_status, metrics_status, deep_review_status, performance_class, confidence, skill_extraction_status, unresolved_reason.

## Performance Classes
VIRAL_VERIFIED: comparative performance evidence supports exceptional reach/engagement.
BREAKOUT_REPORTED: creator/platform/public source reports a breakout but full comparable metrics unavailable.
STRONG_RELATIVE: stronger than relevant local baseline with evidence.
ORDINARY_OR_BASELINE: no exceptional evidence.
WEAK_RELATIVE: weaker than relevant baseline with evidence.
FAILURE_REPORTED: creator/team explicitly documents the attempt as weak/failed.
CONTROVERSIAL: meaningful dispute/negative response supported by evidence.
UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence.

Never infer failure from low absolute views across different eras/platforms.

- casting: novelty/empathy/representativeness/introspection
- listener submission supply loop
- producer-as-director workflow
- interview/field collection
- transcript and structure reconstruction
- narrator/host restraint
- sound-space design
- title testing/discussion
- internal listening/rejection
- free/paid/member/video/brand format evolution
- viral/ordinary/weak/controversial cases

## 凉子访谈录 Research Dimensions
- 2020 early experimental form
- reported weak first 20+ episodes: vertical, single-camera, under-one-minute, shallow emotional layering
- story-led/deeper interview pivot
- horizontal/more continuous edit and 1–3 minute form
- reported 300k early small breakout, then million and ten-million breakout milestones
- subject sourcing/casting
- trust and interview ethics
- question ladders and follow-ups
- viral subject/episode analysis
- ordinary/weak/failure analysis
- packaging and multi-platform adaptation
- creator-IP evolution

## Anti-selection Bias
For every high-performing cluster, deliberately sample nearby ordinary/weak cases. Compare same era, platform, format and audience context when possible.

## Skill Extraction Rule
A mechanism is not promoted as a reusable Skill merely because one viral case contains it. Require cross-case support or label HYPOTHESIS. Record negative cases where the same mechanism failed.

## Pending Ledger Rule
Missing cases stay visible. Do not delete or silently skip inaccessible episodes. Record why research cannot proceed.

## Copyright Rule
Store structured analysis and short necessary excerpts only. Do not archive complete copyrighted transcripts/audio/video unless separately authorized.
