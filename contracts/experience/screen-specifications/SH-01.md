## 1. Product invariant and scope

SH-01 is the Shared Capability catalog consumed by CTH and BMP. It is not a third product, and catalog membership does not establish a CTH Subject or BMP BrandFocus. The App displays safe registered capability metadata from the shared catalog. It does not execute a capability.

The current global content Evidence list and detail routes do not bind the authenticated actor to a product domain or an Evidence object. The App must not call them, expose their records or totals, or attribute them to CTH/BMP. The detail response also forwards arbitrary structured output that could echo a prompt or input. Output remains hidden until object scope and output redaction are verified.
