# Zachary AI Chat / Architecture Sync — 2026-09-01

## Product boundary

Zachary AI is an independent general-purpose AI core. Z-Brand-Core is a product/domain environment through which Zachary may be evaluated and matured; Z-Brand architecture, business rules and implementation remain separate.

## Decisions

- Zachary remains internal-only during the current maturation stage.
- Brand/product-specific knowledge, workflows, schemas, UI, permissions and business rules remain DOMAIN-BOUND.
- General reasoning patterns can become transfer candidates only after evidence and cross-domain validation.
- Domain memory must never implicitly become global memory.
- Product success alone cannot promote a capability to Zachary Core.
- Learning must be evaluated through outcome improvement, repair, transfer, regression and isolation.
- Future standalone Zachary extraction is an explicit maturity decision by the owner.

This is a synchronization log only; it does not change Z-Brand-Core behavior or authorize product-code changes.
