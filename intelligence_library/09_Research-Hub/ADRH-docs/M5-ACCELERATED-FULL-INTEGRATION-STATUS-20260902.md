The relationship is intentionally bidirectional:

- ZBC → Zachary: approved domain data, cases, evidence, system knowledge and real-use results cultivate Zachary.
- Zachary → ZBC: validated research, discoveries, capabilities, reasoning, UX/product and engineering improvements may return to ZBC through governed promotion.

No direct uncontrolled bidirectional writes are permitted.
