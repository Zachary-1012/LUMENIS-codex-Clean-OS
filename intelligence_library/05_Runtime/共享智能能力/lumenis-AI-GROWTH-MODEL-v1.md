# Zachary AI Growth Model v1

## 1. Core definition

Zachary AI is an independent AI core. Products are application, validation, and real-world learning environments; they are not Zachary's identity or permanent capability modules.

The objective is to mature Zachary through real product development, successful deployment, real usage, evidence, evaluation, and controlled capability promotion.

## 2. Product relationship

```text
Zachary AI (independent core)
        |
        +-- Z-Brand-Core / brand & content domain
        +-- SICI-Core / product interaction domain
        +-- CTH / future domain products
        +-- other future products
        |
        +-- Research-Hub / knowledge, cases, research, evidence
        +-- DCC / audit, testing, gates, release control
```

A product may teach Zachary domain-specific knowledge and expose new capability requirements, but a product feature must not automatically become a Zachary capability.

## 3. Learning loop

```text
Need
 -> Understand
 -> Retrieve knowledge/evidence
 -> Reason
 -> Candidate strategy/action
 -> Execute in product
 -> Real-world outcome
 -> Evaluate
 -> Capture experience
 -> Research / benchmark
 -> Shadow validation
 -> Evidence
 -> Capability candidate
 -> Gate / promotion
 -> Zachary capability
 -> Reuse in future products
```

## 4. Knowledge assets

Cases, UGC, brand content, platform research, industry reports, topic signals, emotion research, successful examples, failures, counterexamples and benchmarks are background knowledge assets. They are not required to be exposed as user-facing browsing modules.

Zachary should retrieve them contextually when answering or acting.

## 5. Case principles

- CASE-0001 (autumn × fragrance × emotion) is a real learning sample, not a copywriting template.
- Cases must span categories, audiences, contexts, objectives, topics, platforms and outcomes.
- Positive and negative/counter cases are both required.
- Real human/brand/creator content is preferred; provenance and evidence level must be retained.
- No AI-generated fictional case may be treated as real-world evidence.
- One successful pattern must never be promoted into a universal rule without cross-case validation.

## 6. Capability promotion

Observed behavior -> hypothesis -> candidate capability -> benchmark -> shadow -> evidence -> review -> verified capability.

Zachary must not autonomously rewrite or promote its own core capabilities solely from one successful interaction.

## 7. User experience principle

Zachary is the intelligent interaction surface. Users should be able to state a goal naturally. Zachary decides which knowledge, tools, domain systems and evidence are needed, then returns an integrated answer or action.

The system should avoid forcing users to navigate separate "case library", "emotion library", "tag library" or "copy library" modules merely to obtain intelligence.

## 8. Generalization target

The long-term target is not "an AI that writes Xiaohongshu copy". It is an AI that can understand context, classify intent, reason across evidence, select strategies, execute, evaluate outcomes, learn from success and failure, and reuse verified capabilities across products and domains.

## 9. Safety and quality boundary

Learning must remain evidence-driven and auditable. Deployment feedback can create learning candidates, but promotion requires validation and engineering controls. This keeps continuous improvement separate from uncontrolled self-modification.
