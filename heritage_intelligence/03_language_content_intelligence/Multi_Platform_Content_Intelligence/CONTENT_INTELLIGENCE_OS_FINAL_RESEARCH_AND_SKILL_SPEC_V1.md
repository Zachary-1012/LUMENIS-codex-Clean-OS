## 1. Product Definition

Content Intelligence OS is not an AI copywriter. It is a reusable judgment and production layer that turns real people/events/brands/evidence into platform-native content while preserving facts, ethics and traceability.

Core chain:

Real World → Evidence/Fact Contract → Human Insight → Story/Argument → Narrative Architecture → Platform Router → Production → Editorial/Ethical Gate → Evaluation → Feedback → Learning Asset.

## 2. Three Foundational Engines

### 2.1 Narrative Documentary Engine — StoryFM benchmark
Learn mechanisms, not wording: casting, trust, deep interview, scene extraction, timeline/causality reconstruction, conflict/desire/decision mapping, editorial restructuring, host restraint, sound-space design, internal review and audience-to-story supply loop.

Research evidence indicates StoryFM evolved from the 2016 爱哲电台 precursor to the June 2017 StoryFM launch. Public producer interviews describe producers as director-like owners of sourcing, pre-interview/interview, transcript selection, structural reconstruction, narration, sound cues, internal listening and title discussion. Public profiles describe story selection around novelty/empathy and interviewee introspection rather than eloquence alone.

### 2.2 Deep Interview Engine — 凉子访谈录 benchmark
The benchmark is not a fixed visual style. Its value is the evolution from early weak-performing short vertical single-camera interviews toward story-led, deeper, more continuous 1–3 minute interview edits after 20+ iterations, followed by early 300k, million and ten-million-view breakthroughs reported in its public methodology retrospective.

Extractable chain:
Candidate → Trust → Fact → Scene → Feeling → Conflict → Choice → Reflection → Meaning.

Question ladder:
Fact → Scene/Detail → Feeling → Conflict → Decision → Consequence → Retrospective Meaning.

The system must learn both viral cases and failed/weak experiments. A viral outcome is evidence for investigation, not proof that every mechanism is causal.

### 2.3 Human Wellbeing / Meaning Engine — Harvard Positive Psychology + Happier
Do not reduce “哈佛幸福课” to motivational quotations. The research object includes Tal Ben-Shahar's Positive Psychology 1504 legacy, Happier, and current Harvard/Harvard Medical School positive-psychology materials. Topics include long-term wellbeing, strengths, mindfulness, gratitude, savoring, flow, relationships, self-compassion and meaning. Ben-Shahar's later SPIRE framework is a related modern evolution, but must be distinguished historically from the original Harvard course.

Use in content intelligence:
- Connection: does the content reduce isolation or create recognition?
- Meaning: does it help audiences interpret experience?
- Growth: is there change, learning or agency?
- Strength/Agency: does the subject retain complexity and choice?
- Relationship: are human bonds represented credibly?
- Presence/Experience: are concrete lived moments present rather than abstract slogans?

This is an interpretation/evaluation layer, not a mental-health diagnostic system.

No fabricated metrics. “爆款/失败” requires comparative evidence appropriate to the platform/time period; otherwise mark UNVERIFIED or RELATIVE PERFORMANCE UNKNOWN.

## 4. Viral Intelligence

Viral analysis must separate:
Attention → Retention → Emotional/Cognitive Payoff → Social Transmission → Conversion/Participation → Long-tail Memory.

Candidate mechanisms:
- identity contrast
- high-stakes choice
- hidden world / novelty
- strong recognizability
- unresolved tension
- transformation
- counter-intuitive insight
- quotable but context-supported insight
- audience self-projection
- participation/submission loop

Never equate high views with quality, truth, brand value or long-term trust.

## 5. Failure Intelligence

Failure classes:
F01 Topic-market mismatch
F02 Weak subject/casting
F03 No meaningful change or tension
F04 Abstract answers / missing scenes
F05 Structure/pace failure
F06 Emotional manipulation or emotional flatness
F07 Platform-expression mismatch
F08 Packaging/title mismatch
F09 Brand/commercial intrusion
F10 Fact/credibility failure
F11 Ethical/privacy failure
F12 Repetition/content fatigue
F13 Production-quality obstruction
F14 CTA/conversion mismatch
F15 Strong content but distribution/context disadvantage

A low-performing case is not automatically bad content. Separate Content Quality, Distribution, Timing, Audience Fit and Measurement Quality.

## 6. Multi-platform Router

Same core must be re-authored, not merely shortened.

Podcast: full causal/emotional arc, voice, silence, reflection.
Video documentary/Bilibili: visual evidence + narrative depth + explanatory structure.
Douyin/short video: decisive moment/contrast/tension early, visual progression, compressed payoff.
Xiaohongshu: lived detail, identity/emotion/lifestyle utility, credible facts, social-native rhythm.
WeChat/long-form: context, argument, evidence, narrative continuity.
Zhihu: question/problem framing, reasoning, evidence, experience and counterarguments.
Weibo: event relevance, concise viewpoint, conversational spread and timely context.
Brand content: person × place/product × time × emotion × brand meaning, with factual campaign contract.

## 9. Agent Orchestration

Content Task → Heritage Retrieval → Current Brief → Fact Contract → Research/Evidence → Human Insight → Story/Interview Decision → Narrative Architecture → Platform Route → Draft/Script → Fact Gate → Editorial/Ethics Gate → Rubric → Human Approval → Publish → Performance Evidence → Case Update → Learning Memory.

AI may propose/analyze/draft/evaluate. AI must not invent campaign facts, silently alter dates/prices/rules, impersonate interview evidence, copy protected source material, or autonomously publish without the applicable permission contract.

## 10. Evaluation Model

Separate scores:
- Fact Integrity
- Human Insight
- Story/Narrative Strength
- Interview Depth
- Scene Specificity
- Emotional Credibility
- Meaning/Reflection
- Platform Native Fit
- Brand Fit
- Distinctiveness
- Anti-cliche / Anti-AI texture
- Ethical/Privacy Safety
- Packaging Strength
- Task Effectiveness
- Evidence/Traceability

Hard fail: fabricated material fact; materially wrong campaign rule; deceptive quotation/interview attribution; unlicensed substantial copying; severe privacy/ethical violation; platform/compliance violation.

## 11. Case Coverage Governance

For StoryFM and 凉子访谈录, “全量” means coverage is auditable, not that incomplete research is declared complete.

Statuses:
DISCOVERED → METADATA_VERIFIED → SOURCE_VERIFIED → DEEP_REVIEWED → CROSS_CASE_COMPARED → SKILL_EXTRACTED → REVALIDATED.

Pending reasons:
SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE / TRANSCRIPT_UNAVAILABLE / METRICS_UNAVAILABLE / COPYRIGHT_LIMIT / NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE / DUPLICATE_OR_REPOST / PLATFORM_REMOVED.

No case may disappear because it cannot yet be deeply reviewed.

## 13. Final Review Criteria

This research line is mature enough for product review only when:
- StoryFM and 凉子 have auditable inventories and visible Pending Ledgers;
- viral, ordinary, weak/failure and controversy cases are represented without fabricated metrics;
- Harvard positive psychology/Happier concepts are source-distinguished and not conflated;
- every major abstraction is supported by multiple cases or marked hypothesis;
- cross-platform transformations are tested on the same content core;
- Content Director skills have input/output/contracts/failure tests;
- copyright/privacy/ethics boundaries are explicit;
- research assets remain isolated from Production Truth until promotion.

## 14. Current Conclusion

The target is a Human Content Intelligence system: it learns why content works, when it fails, how humans construct meaning, how interviewers surface lived experience, how producers organize narrative, and how the same truth should change form across platforms. Its competitive value is judgment + evidence + reusable production method, not imitation.
