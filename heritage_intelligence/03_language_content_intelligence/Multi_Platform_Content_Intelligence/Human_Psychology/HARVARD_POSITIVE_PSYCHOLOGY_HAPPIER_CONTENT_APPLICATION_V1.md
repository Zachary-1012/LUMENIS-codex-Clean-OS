# Harvard Positive Psychology / Happier → Content Intelligence Application V1

> Research boundary: source-grounded conceptual learning, not clinical advice and not a substitute for mental-health expertise.

## Historical distinction
Do not call every later happiness framework “the Harvard happiness course.”

Research objects are separated:
1. Tal Ben-Shahar's Harvard Positive Psychology 1504 era/course legacy (mid-2000s).
2. Ben-Shahar's book Happier, which applies positive-psychology research to happiness, goals, education, work and relationships.
3. Current Harvard/Harvard Medical School positive-psychology learning materials, which include strengths, mindfulness, gratitude, savoring, flow, relationships, self-compassion and meaning.
4. Ben-Shahar's later Happiness Studies / SPIRE work as a subsequent evolution, not retroactively identical to the original Harvard course.

## What LUMENIS should learn
Content should not merely maximize arousal. Evaluate whether it supports durable human value:

### Connection
Does the audience recognize themselves or another person more fully?

### Meaning
Does the narrative help interpret experience rather than merely stimulate emotion?

### Growth and Agency
Does the person retain choices, learning, strengths and complexity rather than being reduced to victim/hero labels?

### Presence and Concrete Experience
Does the work contain lived moments, sensory detail and attention rather than abstract inspirational language?

### Relationships
Are relationships represented as consequential parts of wellbeing and identity?

### Gratitude / Savoring / Flow / Strengths
When relevant to the actual case, these can be analytical lenses. They must not be inserted mechanically into every story.

### Permission for difficult emotion
Positive psychology is not a mandate for forced positivity. Content evaluation should penalize toxic positivity and emotional simplification.

## Content Evaluation Questions
- What human need or experience is present?
- What changes in the person's understanding, agency or relationship?
- Is meaning earned by the evidence/story or imposed by the writer?
- Is pain being exploited for attention?
- Does the audience receive recognition, insight, agency, connection or useful reflection?
- Does the ending resolve too neatly compared with the facts?

## Failure Anti-patterns
- motivational slogan replacing evidence
- forced healing arc
- suffering-as-spectacle
- universal advice from one anecdote
- happiness = constant positive emotion
- moralizing a person's coping choices
- using psychological terminology as diagnosis

## Transfer to StoryFM / 凉子 / Brand Content
StoryFM: use wellbeing lenses to interpret reflection and resonance, never to rewrite the subject's reality.
凉子: use lenses to design deeper questions around agency, relationships, change and meaning.
Brand: move from “make consumers happy” claims toward real experiences of place, connection, memory, participation and identity when evidence supports them.
