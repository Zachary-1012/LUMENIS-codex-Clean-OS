## 1. Evidence and status policy

- E4: primary/official source or peer-reviewed/archival source with direct evidence.
- E3: strong scholarly secondary source or systematic review.
- E2: credible but incomplete/indirect evidence.
- E1: exploratory lead requiring verification.
- E0: unverified hypothesis/idea.

States: FACT / VERIFIED / PENDING / CONCEPT / RECOMMENDATION / LEGAL REVIEW / LEGACY / REJECT.

## 2. Philosophy and relational becoming

### Finding A — Relational Becoming is an established research term, not an original coined term
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E4/E3  

The phrase “relational becoming” appears in established philosophical literature. A 2006 paper in *Studies in History and Philosophy of Modern Physics* treats relational becoming as a distinct notion in the philosophy of time. A 2022 paper in *Dialogic Pedagogy* uses the term for an ethico-ontological process in which people become certain kinds of persons collectively and individually through interaction. A 2025 SSRN paper further connects relational ontology, creativity, emergence and becoming.

**Implication:** The concept can be used as an internal research descriptor, but must not be represented as an original proprietary philosophical term of 序境.

Sources:
- https://doi.org/10.1016/S1355-2198(06)00026-8
- https://doi.org/10.5195/dpj.2022.459
- https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5242300

### Finding B — Relation, emergence and self-organization provide a defensible bridge from micro to macro
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E3/E4

Recent complexity research describes self-organization and emergence across physics, chemistry, biology, ecology, robotics, AI, linguistics, social science and engineering. A 2025 *npj Complexity* review emphasizes that macroscopic properties can depend on how components interact and organize, not merely on component-level properties. Reviews of emergence also emphasize cross-level interactions and the difficulty of deriving macro-level properties solely from micro-level descriptions.

**Implication for philosophy:** The modern interpretation of “芥子纳须弥” can be framed as a philosophical metaphor about meaningful units, relations and emergent wholes; it should not be presented as a scientific theorem.

Sources:
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s44260-025-00031-5
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10887681/
- https://doi.org/10.1080/10919392.2020.1748977

## 3. Life systems and complexity

### Finding C — Current systems-biology research explicitly studies emergence across chemical, informational and ecological levels
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E3

A 2026 *Current Opinion in Systems Biology* review surveys systems-level models of life's origins and describes progressive emergence of complexity across interconnected chemical, informational and ecological levels, including shared dynamical principles across levels.

**Implication:** The Life-level layer in the 序境 worldview can legitimately research cross-scale organization and emergence, while avoiding unsupported claims that the product itself is a “living system.”

Source:
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.coisb.2026.100588

## 4. AI, agents, embodied intelligence and robotics

### Finding D — Embodied AI is increasingly structured around perception, decision and execution
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E3

A 2025 survey in *SmartBot* organizes Embodied AI around embodied perception, embodied decision-making and embodied execution. A 2026 *Robotics* systematic review describes foundation-model-based embodied AI for mobile service robots as combining perception, reasoning and action in dynamic environments. Recent reviews also emphasize remaining gaps between foundation-model reasoning and physical control/manipulation.

**Implication for future AI architecture research:** The existing Reality → Evidence → Understanding → Intelligence → Decision → Action → Feedback → Evolution abstraction is compatible as a conceptual research model, but must not be claimed to reproduce embodied robotics architecture without implementation evidence.

Sources:
- https://doi.org/10.1002/smb2.70003
- https://doi.org/10.3390/robotics15030055
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rcim.2026.103269

### Finding E — Memory, feedback and adaptation are important in complex adaptive systems
**Status:** VERIFIED / CONCEPT BRIDGE  
**Evidence:** E3

Research on complex adaptive systems describes agents interacting with environments, updating internal models through feedback, and generating system-level adaptive behavior. This supports researching memory and feedback as mechanisms for adaptive intelligence, while the exact architecture must be empirically tested in the self-developed system.

Sources:
- https://technav.ieee.org/topic/complex-adaptive-systems/
- https://www.mdpi.com/2079-8954/12/2/45

## 5. Multilingual and cultural intelligence

### Finding F — Multilingual LLMs can retain language-specific cultural knowledge that does not automatically surface
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E4

ACL 2026 research finds that multilingual LLMs can exhibit an explicit-implicit localization gap: cultural knowledge may exist in the model but not naturally surface without explicit cultural context. The study reports gaps above 10% in the majority of investigated models and shows that explicit cultural context improves localization.

**Implication:** The AI should not use direct translation as the default localization strategy. It should model language, culture, context and task separately.

Source:
- https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2141/

### Finding G — Cultural grounding needs evaluation across language and modality
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E4

ACL 2026 MMAC introduces a multilingual, multimodal cultural-grounding evaluation framework covering cross-country awareness disparities, cross-lingual consistency, cross-modal consistency, knowledge generalization and grounding validity. The benchmark contains 27,000 human-curated questions across 10 languages.

**Implication:** Future ZH/EN/KO content intelligence should evaluate not only translation correctness but cultural grounding and cross-language consistency.

Source:
- https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.989/

### Finding H — Multilingual reasoning benefits from language- and culture-specific context
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E4

ACL 2026 EMCEE reports that extracting and merging language-specific synthetic context can improve multilingual performance, especially in low-resource languages, supporting a design in which language-specific context is explicitly retrieved rather than assuming English-centric latent knowledge is sufficient.

Source:
- https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.127/

## 6. Semantic Gravity — terminology discipline

### Finding I — “Semantic gravity” has established technical uses and must not be claimed as proprietary by default
**Status:** VERIFIED  
**Evidence:** E4/E3

In education and multimodal communication research, semantic gravity refers to the degree to which meaning depends on context. This is a recognized scholarly concept. The phrase is also used by other contemporary authors in broader conceptual frameworks.

**Implication:** The 序境 research framework may use “Semantic Gravity / 语义引力” as an internal analytical label only after defining its specific operational meaning. It should not be presented as an established scientific law or as an original invention unless separately established.

Sources:
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.system.2021.102561
- https://thejamesshen.com/semantic-gravity-ai-meaning-attraction/

## 10. Direct applications to the self-developed AI

Use cultural context, emerging language, narrative patterns, audience vocabulary and semantic shifts as research signals rather than relying only on keyword frequency.

## 12. Research conclusion — current synthesis

The strongest validated bridge to the 序境 worldview is not the claim that “the whole is simply contained inside the part.” A more defensible formulation is:

> Meaningful units participate in relations; relations organize into structures; structures can generate properties that are not visible at the isolated component level; feedback and adaptation can alter subsequent states.

This gives a rigorous research analogue for the philosophical proposition:

> **芥子纳须弥：微小之中本身就包含通向整体的结构。**

The AI should therefore learn the distinction between:

**metaphor → theory → empirical finding → implementation hypothesis.**

That distinction is itself a core part of the Research Intelligence methodology.
