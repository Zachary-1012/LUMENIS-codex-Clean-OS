## 1. Product invariant and scope

SH-01 is the Shared Capability catalog consumed by CTH and BMP. It is not a third product, and catalog membership does not establish a CTH Subject or BMP BrandFocus. The App displays safe registered capability metadata from the shared catalog. It does not execute a capability.

The current global content Evidence list and detail routes do not bind the authenticated actor to a product domain or an Evidence object. The App must not call them, expose their records or totals, or attribute them to CTH/BMP. The detail response also forwards arbitrary structured output that could echo a prompt or input. Output remains hidden until object scope and output redaction are verified.

## 11. Discover inside research and creation — 2026-10-03

Requirement/owner direction accepted by M in
`work/m-controller-20261002/discovery-recommendation-owner-decision-20261003.md`.
The Product Owner requires collected content to support useful trends, observed
hotspots and comparable works, then flow into an actual creative task. This is
the existing SH-01 Discover subview, not another screen ID or a second research
service. The detailed binding/visual candidate remains DESIGN_EXPLORATION until
W6 freezes the real existing callable and M reviews the exact page. Do not ship a
nonfunctional Discover destination or fake recommendation to fill this gap.

### 11.1 Entry, scope and distinct tasks

After a real collection attempt, retain successful records and failure receipts;
opening Discover is deliberate. Carry actual platform/query/time/task context.
AI and creation tasks may enter with selected locatable references. Default
GLOBAL_ALL works without selecting a Subject or BrandFocus; optional FOCUS and
platform/time filters narrow the query. A real server refusal is distinct from
the normal no-focus state. No account/login/focus selection wall.

- Trends asks what changed over comparable observed periods. Use a real series
  and readable explanation; missing observations are not zero.
- Hotspots asks what received observed attention in the known source/corpus.
  Preserve platform-native metrics and coverage; no invented cross-platform score.
- Comparable works asks why an actual work is useful for this task, which
  dimensions match and which differences matter. Popularity alone is not fit.

SH-02 remains source/material reading; BMP-03 remains market interpretation.
They may open the same locatable references without becoming three copies of
the same feed. Original-platform facts do not prescribe the product's layout.

### 11.2 Functional composition and media reading

Within SH-01, a compact persistent navigation row selects Discover or the actual
existing creation activity. Discover has three named task views. A small filter
line contains only actual platform/time/task/focus controls. No slogan, overview
KPI wall, introductory cards, source-platform chrome or imitation social feed.

Desktop: aligned title/source/time rows or work thumbnails occupy the main
reading area. A selected item opens a bounded reasoning/source area while
retaining the list and filters. Numeric observations align with their units;
small real brand/platform marks or honest initials support recognition. Missing
logos do not trigger a remote tracking request or invented brand image.

Mobile: selecting an item opens a reversible detail page/layer, not a long stack
of every field. Back/Close restores selection, filters, scroll and focus. Images
use their real dimensions with deliberate enlargement; video/audio use actual
returned media through the existing media owner and explicit playback controls.
No fake waveforms, guessed duration, auto-playing remote feed or platform page
embedded as the normal product result. Text-only intake cannot imply unseen
image/video/audio analysis. Reading text, analysis and original-source metadata
retain separate headings only where the distinction is meaningful.

### 11.3 Required real fields and actions

W6 supplies the existing callable names/methods/statuses before binding; this
specification invents none. Any unknown action stays out of production until
its current owner is connected, while independent reading/creation work continues.

### 11.4 States, recovery and acceptance

Keep loading, no records, not collected, insufficient observations, partial
platform success, media unavailable, owned inference unavailable, actual refusal,
offline and failed save distinct. Show retained usable material when lawful.
Switching layers does not cancel or repeat work. A real retry is bounded and
preserves intent; revocation/scope changes reject stale forbidden material.

Required real operation: actual collection result -> Discover with exact scope
and failures -> one justified result and supporting text/media -> selected
references -> existing editable plan/video/podcast -> save/version/reopen ->
evaluation/return. Cover default global, optional focus/clear, narrow/wide layouts,
zh-CN/en/ko, 200% text, keyboard, reduced motion and vivid wallpapers. Static
source, examples and widget tests do not accept this workflow. Every unavailable
dependency remains separately named; no global coverage or release claim.
